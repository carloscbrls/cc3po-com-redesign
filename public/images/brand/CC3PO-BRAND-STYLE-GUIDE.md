## 5. Portrait Style (Agent Avatars)

### Overall Aesthetic
The existing 24 agent portraits + Carlos portrait use **photorealistic AI-generated human portraits** in a professional/headshot style. Key characteristics:

- **Medium:** Photorealistic digital render (AI-generated)
- **Framing:** Chest-up to shoulders-up medium close-ups
- **Lighting:** Controlled studio lighting with directional quality; warm to neutral color temperature
- **Background:** Two distinct approaches:
  - **Environmental portraits** (~60%): Blurred urban, library, workspace backgrounds with bokeh
  - **Studio headshots** (~40%): Solid or gradient dark neutral background
- **Clothing:** Dark, muted professional attire (navy, charcoal, black, olive) — no bright colors
- **Expression:** Direct eye contact with viewer; confident, professional range from contemplative to warm
- **Skin texture:** Naturalistic with visible pores and subtle imperfections
- **Depth:** Shallow DOF with soft bokeh in environmental shots

### DO NOT
- Use cartoon/illustrated style portraits
- Use bright saturated clothing
- Use white or light backgrounds
- Use generic stock-style flat lighting
- Break direct eye contact pattern
- Add text overlays on portraits

### DO
- Maintain photorealistic quality
- Use dark/muted backgrounds with selective bokeh
- Keep professional attire in dark neutrals
- Ensure direct eye contact
- Match warm-to-neutral color temperature
- Include subtle environmental storytelling

## 6. Logo Direction

### Concept
- **Style:** Geometric, circuit-inspired, dark background
- **Colors:** Primary cyan `#00d2ff` + purple `#6b35ff` gradient
- **Elements:** Circuit traces, grid patterns, the "C" or "CC3PO" rendered in monospace (JetBrains Mono)
- **Background:** Dark `#0a0a0b` or transparent
- **Feel:** Tech-forward but approachable, systems-oriented, connected

### Card Structure (Agent Cards)
- Padding: 24px 20px 20px
- Gap between cards: 16px (mobile), 20px (desktop)
- Card grid: `minmax(200px, 1fr)` → `minmax(220px, 1fr)` at 768px+
- Top border: 2px gradient (department color), expands to 3px on hover
- Hover: translateY(-4px), background shift, glow shadow

## 9. Background Effects

### Particle Canvas
- 50 particles, colors cycling between purple/cyan/gold
- Particle radius: 0.5-2px, alpha: 0.08-0.33
- Speed: ±0.3px/frame
- Connection lines within 150px distance, cyan `rgba(0,210,255,0.06)` fading with distance

### Grid Overlay
- CSS grid pattern: `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.02)` at 60px intervals
- Covers entire viewport, `pointer-events: none`
- Z-index: 0 (behind content at z-index: 2)

### Section Gradients
- Header: `radial-gradient(ellipse at center top, rgba(107,53,255,0.08) 0%, transparent 60%)`
- Footer: `radial-gradient(ellipse at center bottom, rgba(0,210,255,0.1) 0%, transparent 60%)`

## 10. Animation Guidelines

| Animation | Duration | Easing | Usage |
|-----------|----------|--------|-------|
| `gradientShift` | 8s | ease infinite | Hero title gradient |
| `pulse-dot` | 2s | ease infinite | Status indicator |
| `emoji-float` | 2s | ease infinite | Card emoji on hover |
| Card hover | 0.3s | ease | translateY(-4px) |

### Workday Animation
- Sequential reveal of steps, 1200ms per step
- Each step: opacity 0→1, translateY(20px→0), transition 0.5s ease-out
